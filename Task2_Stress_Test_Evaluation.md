To test FinBuddy's system prompt using six questions and evaluate whether it follows its rules.

Critical Evaluation

FinBuddy followed most of its instructions during the stress test. It used beginner-friendly language, avoided personal investment recommendations, handled private financial information cautiously, and explained investment uncertainty.

The SBI interest-rate response was the main area requiring improvement because financial rates can change and should be verified from the official bank source before being presented as current.

Conclusion

The stress test showed that FinBuddy was generally helpful, clear, and cautious. Version 2 improved privacy and investment safety, but financial information that changes over time should always be checked against official sources.
