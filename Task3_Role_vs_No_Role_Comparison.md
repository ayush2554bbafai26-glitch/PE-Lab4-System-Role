Lab 4 – Task 3: Role Prompting Comparison

Objective

To compare how different role instructions change AI responses to the same financial question.

Question used

Should a 19-year-old take a credit card?

Comparison Table

Prompt Type| Accuracy| Tone| Caution
No Role| Provided a balanced explanation of benefits, risks, repayment responsibility, and credit card discipline.| Neutral and informative.| Explained risks such as interest, late fees, overspending, and debt.
Experienced Credit Analyst| Gave a structured analysis of credit history, repayment ability, income, fees, risks, and financial responsibility.| Professional and analytical.| Strong emphasis on income, repayment ability, and understanding credit terms.
Friendly College Senior| Explained credit cards in a simple, relatable way using student-friendly language and practical examples.| Friendly, casual, and approachable.| Warned against overspending and advised using credit only when repayment is manageable.

Reflection (100 Words)

Role prompting changes the style, structure, and focus of an AI response. Without a role, the AI provides a general explanation covering the main benefits and risks. When given the role of an experienced credit analyst, it becomes more professional and focuses on repayment ability, fees, credit history, and financial risks. When assigned the role of a friendly college senior, the explanation becomes more relatable and easier for students to understand. The basic information may remain similar, but the presentation changes significantly. Role prompting is useful for adapting answers to different audiences, although facts still need independent verification.

Conclusion

The comparison shows that role prompting mainly influences tone, perspective, and the organization of an answer. Choosing an appropriate role can make AI responses more useful for a specific audience.
